About Me

A practice built on attention

An engaging Twitter content creator, a recording creator, an article writer, and a fine artist.

Asma Akter photographs with a patient eye and prepares high-quality pictures for Shutterstock. Capturing moments, creating art, and writing sit beside that work, each one a way of keeping what she sees.

On Twitter she builds an engaging presence around new pictures and ideas. In recordings she gives a story a voice. In articles she writes with clarity and warmth. As a fine artist she develops original work, including pieces recognized in art competitions.

The standard is the same across all four: a picture clear enough to license, a sentence clear enough to remember, and a recording worth hearing to the end.

Twitter Content Creator

Engaging posts and conversations that bring new pictures, art, and writing to readers.

Recording Creator

Recorded stories shaped with care, so the work can be heard as well as seen.

Article Writer

Articles and essays in a clear, personal voice, often circling memory, making, and daily life.

Fine Artist

Original art developed with patience, including work awarded in art competitions.
